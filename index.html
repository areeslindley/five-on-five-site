A statistical match centre

Hockey, taken apart

Five on Five is the NHL sister to Down & Distance: results, xG, rest and win-probability models, treated as samples rather than recaps and scored against a closing-line baseline. Slot 1 is spent. MoneyPuck is credited on every page that uses a shot.

What this is

Each finished game is a sample: a score, Elo, Dixon–Coles and logistic-regression home-win chances, a 5v5 xG pair, and whether it went past regulation. Collect those samples and a club or a season is a distribution, not a recap.

Skater and goalie logs come from MoneyPuck listed files — documented, versioned, non-commercial with credit. Not a scraped vendor, and not the NHL API.

How to read it

Season is one year at a glance. Scores is one day's games with the Elo and Dixon–Coles win probabilities. Standings is the table on any date. Games is every result, filterable. Teams and Structure follow each year's real conferences and divisions. Players is goalie GSAx. Years labels lockout, realignment, 3-on-3 OT, Vegas, COVID, Seattle.

The closing line is the yardstick on the Predictions page, scored in aggregate. No game's closing price or posted total is published on this site.

The close

C0 is the posted close, not a learned model. Elo, Dixon–Coles and a logistic regression are lab models, not registered. Slot 1 shades the posted total from schedule density and did not clear.

Walk-forward lab

Predictions

The closing line wins. The evidence is a reliability diagram, a Brier split on that same scale, every eval season as a row, sequential Elo and Dixon–Coles as lab models, the residual probe including what failed, and slot 1 — a totals shade that spends alpha whether or not it clears.

The closing-line archive behind these figures (Sportsbook Reviews Online, 2008–09 to 2022–23) is kept off this site. Everything below is an aggregate: Brier scores, reliability bins, per-season rows and slot results. No game's closing price or posted total is published.

What is published
Published and ledger
C0 close
Shin (1993) on the two-way closing moneyline. P(home win) is vig-free. There is no draw class. Scoreline is posted total plus puck line where both exist from 2014-15. Regulation three-way is not posted and is not modelled here. This is not a learned model.
Ledger
K_MAX is 10. Slot 1 is spent: a walk-forward shade of the posted total on combined games in the last seven days. It did not clear the O/U gate. P(home) stays C0. Nine slots remain.
Lab only — not ledger tests
Dixon–Coles
Bivariate Poisson on ENG-adjusted regulation goals (team attack and defence, an era scoring level and an era home edge), then P(home) = P(reg) + P(tie) × P(home | tie). Previous-game starter GSAx is subtracted from the opponent λ. Walk-forward vs C0 on the Predictions card. Lab, not a ledger test. 2008 has no prior-season fit.
Elo
Sequential ratings. Walk-forward pick is K = 8, season-regression α = 0.2, home ice = 35 Elo points. Frozen FiveThirtyEight constants (K = 8, α = 1/3, home ice = 50) are the 2008 default. Lab, not a ledger test.
Logistic regression
A regularised logistic regression on the Elo probability plus rest, back-to-backs, travel, previous-game goalie GSAx, opponent-adjusted xG, PDO and special teams, all as of the morning of the game. No price is an input, so its per-game chances are published. Each season is predicted by a fit on earlier seasons, with the penalty chosen on those seasons. Walk-forward vs C0 and Elo on the Predictions card. Lab, not a ledger test.
GSAx, xG, PDO, rest
Phase 2 engines. Several predict raw wins. None of the moneyline features cleared the closing-line residual with era-stable sign.
Pick accuracy

Winner-pick accuracy at a 0.5 threshold. Binary Brier remains the headline.

Why the close wins

The market is already calibrated. Walk-forward Platt returns the same Brier. Beating the close needs information it does not already have.

Daily game-day blocks. Shin locked before the walk.

Reliability diagram

Stated P(home) against how often the home side won. The dashed line is perfect calibration. Caption is on the binary Brier scale.

Brier split

Binary Murphy: Brier = calibration + refinement, all on the headline scale. ECE is in probability points.

Per season

The current year is one row. The close window is the whole table, not the latest MoneyPuck file.

What the close does not already know

HC1 OLS of the closing-line residual. Moves residual = overall p < 0.05 and sign-stable across eras. Failures are the finding.

Dixon–Coles · lab

Bivariate Poisson on ENG-adjusted regulation goals, with previous-game starter GSAx in the λs. Not registered. The close is still the baseline.

Logistic regression · lab

Regularised logistic regression on Elo, rest, travel, goalie and xG inputs. No price is an input. Each season is predicted by a fit on earlier seasons only. Not registered. The close is still the baseline.

Elo · lab

Sequential Elo with a walk-forward home-ice boost. K and α are chosen on prior seasons. Not registered. The close is still the baseline.

C1 · slot 1

Walk-forward shade of the posted total on combined games in the last seven days. P(home) stays C0. The lead number is one-sided O/U vs a coin-flip at spent α. The slot is spent either way.
